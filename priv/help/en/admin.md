# For admins

## Settings

**Settings** (in your profile, Admin) holds:

- **Sign-in options**: switch Google, Facebook, Instagram or Telegram sign-in on or off.
- **Search distance**: how renters choose how far around them to search.
- **Photo checks**: the level (1 Light or 2 Standard; a higher level downloads larger models once and checks more precisely), the progress while a level is prepared, and **Check all photos again**.
- **Outside services**: switch off a service that fails or isn't wanted. BASA keeps working without it, and catches up when it is back.

## Daily work

- **Offer review**: offers the checks sent to an admin.
- **Photo checks**: photos hidden or sent for a look. **Keep** shows the photo again; **Remove** hides it for good. Your decision stays when photos are checked again.
- **Rules**: the rules behind offer and photo checks; open one with **Edit** to change it.
- **Import**: give users access to import and publish the user agreement.
- **Contracts**: check contracts and handle requests from authorities.
