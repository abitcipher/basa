# Offer your space or gear

Everything starts in **My offers**. The steps are numbered there.

1. **Items**: add each piece of gear with photos. Several photos at once are fine; the first is the cover. An item without photos may get suggested pictures; they are always shown as illustrations with their author.
2. **Rooms**: add the place. Give it a name, find the city, write the address and drag the pin to the entrance.
   - Under **How to find it (optional)** add up to 3 photos of the entrance or street. Renters see them under the map.
   - Where available, **Find street views near the pin** offers street photos; pick one only if it shows your building.
   - Room photos must show the real space.
3. **Baskets**: group the items (and people, such as a sound engineer) that are rented together.
4. **Offers**: pick a basket and a room, set the opening hours, the price (the slider suggests one) and the booking rules, then publish.

When you publish, BASA checks the offer. It goes live at once, goes to an admin first, or comes back as a draft with the reasons shown on **My offers**.

You can leave a step and come back: what you typed stays, also after reloading the page or switching the language.
