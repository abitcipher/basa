# Your account

- **Sign in** with email and password, or with Google, Facebook, Instagram or Telegram where the site offers them.
- **Language**: pick it in the top bar. The page you are on stays as it is.
- **Appearance**: in your profile menu choose button style, colours and sizes.
- **Telegram**: in the BASA bot, the menu under the message field has Find, Bookings, Messages, My offers, Add item and Help; send a photo to add an item. The **BASA** button next to the message field opens the app inside Telegram, starting with large buttons for each part. Tap **Verify phone** once to confirm your number. If you joined through Telegram, add an email and password in your **Profile**, so you can always sign in.
