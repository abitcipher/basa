# Find and book

1. Open **Find**. Type what you need and pick a city, or press the location button in the city field to search around you; then set how far with the distance control.
2. Open an offer to see its photos, what is included and **Where** it is. Under the map you may find photos of the way in, taken by the owner.
3. Under **Pick a time**, choose a day in the calendar, then a free time. Days are marked free, few left or fully booked.
4. Press **Book**. You can add a message to the lessor.
   - **Instant booking**: the booking is confirmed at once.
   - Otherwise the lessor confirms your request; the time is held for you meanwhile.
5. If someone books the same time a moment before you, BASA tells you right away: pick another time.

Your bookings are under **Bookings** → **I booked**. There you can cancel a booking, and once it is confirmed, open its **Contract**.

To ask the lessor something first, press **Message the lessor** on the offer.
