# Import from your website

If you already list your gear on your own website or a classifieds site, BASA can turn those pages into draft items. Nothing is published until you make an offer.

1. An admin gives you access. Then open **My offers** → **Import** and confirm the user agreement.
2. Add a source: a name and up to 5 example pages of the site.
3. Check the tabs:
   - **Found**: what BASA reads from a page by itself.
   - **Pick**: click a value on the page preview to show BASA where it is.
   - **Catalogue**: the list pages BASA should go through to find all items.
   - **Test**: try the setup on your example pages and import them.
4. Imported items appear in your items as drafts. Check them before you use them in an offer.

Importing a page again updates its item instead of adding a new one. Sites that block automated visits can't be imported.
