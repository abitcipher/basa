# Photos

**My offers** → **Photos** shows all photos of your items and rooms in folders, one per item and room. Photos are named after their folder. Delete a photo there or in the item or room form; you stay in the folder you were in.

## Checks

Every photo is checked when it is added. Most photos simply appear. A photo that may break the rules is shown to an admin, who keeps or removes it. A photo that clearly breaks them is hidden at once.

Hidden photos are listed on **My offers** under **Hidden photos**, with the reason. Renters don't see them, and an offer that uses one goes back to draft. Remove or replace the photo; if the check got it wrong, an admin can bring it back.

Room photos must show the real space; for the way in, use the photos under **How to find it**.
