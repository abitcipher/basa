# Your schedule

**My offers** → **Schedule** shows every booking of all your offers. Choose a view at the top:

- **Calendar**: bookings by day, week or month. Click a booking to see it and act on it.
- **By room and gear**: one line for each room, item and person, so you see what is busy when. A line marks the current time.
- **Requests**: cards for waiting, confirmed and finished bookings. Move a card to confirm or decline a request.

Requests also appear under **Bookings** → **For my offers**, with **Confirm**, **Decline** and **Mark as done**.

The colour key is behind the "i". The view and the date you chose stay when you come back.
