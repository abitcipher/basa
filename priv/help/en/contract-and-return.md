# Contract, handover and return

A confirmed booking becomes a contract between you and the other party. BASA keeps the contract and the photos you add, and can prove later that nothing was changed.

## Sign

1. In **Bookings**, open the booking and press **Contract**.
2. Enter your legal name as on your identity document.
3. Press **Connect this device**: the contract is signed on this device. Open the contract later on the same device.
4. Read the contract and press **Agree and sign**. When both parties have signed, the contract is sealed.

## Handover and return (rental)

1. At the handover, add photos of the condition under **Evidence** (**Add photos**).
2. When you return the item, add photos of the condition you return it in.
3. The lessor either presses **Accept the return** or makes a **Claim** within the claim days stated in the contract.
4. If you don't agree with a claim, press **Do not agree** and describe why, or **Declare a dispute**.

A purchase works the same way: the buyer confirms the delivery, then has the claim days stated in the contract.

## Prove it later

Keep the contract's code (the BASA-HASH). For any file there is **Share with proof**: it downloads the file with everything needed to prove it is unchanged. Anyone can check such a file at **/verify**, without an account.
